# Question
Starting in the top left corner of a 2x2 grid,
and only being able to move to the right and down, there are exactly
routes to the bottom right corner.
How many such routes are there through a 20x20
grid?

## Logic
For an $nxn$ grid the overall vector must be (n,n)
The at any point 2 options are available right and down.

Think of having a list of $2n$ items. *The full vector laid out as a list*
These can be arranged in $(2n)!$ different ways.
But there are duplicates, there are *2* sets of *n* objects that are identical.
Example for the 2x2 case, here is a pattern $DRDR$
in this pattern, and in all the $(2n)!$ patterns,
The D's and the R's have $_{n}P_{n}$ permutations counted each.
To counter this, we divide by $n!$ twice, once for each unique item:

$Paths = \frac{(2n)!}{n!\cdot n!}$
for n = 20 we have:
Paths = $\frac{40!}{20!^2} = 137846528820$
